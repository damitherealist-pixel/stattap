How it works

Three moments, all while the customer is still there.

STEP 1

Tap

A StatTap card sits on the table, the counter or the front desk. The customer holds their phone to it and your review page opens. No camera, no code to line up.

STEP 2

Rate

One overall star rating, five quick taps on the parts of the visit that matter for your business, and a few words. Then one button copies the review and opens your Google page.

STEP 3

Grow

Every rating lands in your dashboard right away. A weekly report shows what improved, what slipped, and who to follow up with.

Who we serve

The right five questions for every kind of business.

A barbershop customer cares about the cut. An auto shop customer cares about the price matching the quote. Pick your industry and StatTap loads the questions your customers actually talk about.

You can change any question. Need to ask about parking or a specific staff member? Swap it in when you set up your link. Every business gets its own address, like the one shown on the phone.

About StatTap

Built for the places that deserve to be noticed.

Big chains have survey teams and research budgets. The family restaurant, the barber down the street and the two-room clinic don't, and they are often the ones doing the best work.

StatTap gives every local business the same picture of what customers think, in real time, for less effort than printing a menu. Happy customers turn into public reviews. Unhappy ones reach the owner directly, so problems get fixed instead of posted.

We work with independent and family-owned businesses, neighborhood main streets, and first-time owners building a reputation from zero.
